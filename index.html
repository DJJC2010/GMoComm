Missouri's Commercial MLS — Built by Commercial Agents, for Commercial Agents

A Better MLS for Commercial Real Estate

MOComm MLS is Missouri's dedicated statewide commercial multiple listing service, created specifically to meet the needs of commercial real estate professionals. Built by commercial agents who understand the unique demands of the industry.

Why Choose MOComm MLS?

Commercial-Focused Platform

No residential clutter. No residential workflows. Just the tools and data commercial professionals need to market, search, and close deals efficiently.

Less Red Tape, More Business

MOComm MLS was built to support commercial transactions without the extensive rules, restrictions, and compliance requirements widely associated with residential MLS systems.

Statewide Exposure

Connect with commercial brokers and agents across Missouri through a single statewide network designed exclusively for commercial listings.

Collaboration That Works

Share opportunities, market listings, and expand your reach with a community of commercial real estate professionals focused on growing business across Missouri.

User Friendly & Mobile Ready

Intuitive interface. Easy to navigate. Access listings and tools anytime, anywhere—from desktop or mobile.
